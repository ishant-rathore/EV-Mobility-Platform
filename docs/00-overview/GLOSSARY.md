# 1. Product Terminology

## EV Mobility Platform

The complete software platform for intelligent EV journey planning, energy estimation, charging recommendation, charging reservation, parking reservation, payment, IoT access, and mobility analytics.

---

## Pay&Park

The product-facing concept of the platform that combines EV charging and smart parking into one reservation and mobility experience.
