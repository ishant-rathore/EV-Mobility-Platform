## 1. Purpose

The Future AI module defines potential AI and machine-learning capabilities that can be added after the deterministic MVP intelligence layer is stable.

The goal is to make the platform progressively more:

- Predictive
- Personalized
- Adaptive
- Efficient
- Context-aware

AI must extend the existing intelligence modules rather than replace their deterministic foundations.
