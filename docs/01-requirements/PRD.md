# EV Mobility Platform — Product Requirements Document

**Product:** Pay&Park — Intelligent EV Mobility, Charging & Smart Parking Platform  
**Project:** EV Mobility Platform  
**SIH:** Problem Statement #08 — EV Mobility Platform  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** SIH 2026 MVP

---

## 1. Product Vision

> Make every EV journey more predictable by connecting battery-aware routing, charging intelligence, parking reservation, payment and IoT infrastructure.

---

## 2. Product Promise

> **Plan. Charge. Park. Go.**

---

## 3. Core Problem

EV drivers face fragmented decisions around:

- Range
- Route
- Energy
- Charging
- Waiting
- Cost
- Parking
- Access

Most charger discovery solutions do not connect these decisions into one end-to-end workflow.

---

## 4. Solution

EV Mobility Platform combines:

1. EV profile
2. Battery-aware routing
3. Energy estimation
4. Charging station discovery
5. Station ranking
6. Charging reservation
7. Parking reservation
8. Payment
9. IoT smart parking
10. Analytics

---

## 5. Primary User

### EV Driver

Needs:

- Reliable route
- Battery awareness
- Charging recommendation
- Cost visibility
- Parking availability
- Reservation
- Easy access

---

## 6. Secondary Users

### Charging Station Operator
Needs utilization and station insights.

### Parking Operator
Needs parking occupancy and reservation management.

### Admin
Manages platform configuration and users.
