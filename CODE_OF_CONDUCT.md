# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project community. Contributors must communicate constructively, respect differing experience and viewpoints, protect private information, and avoid discriminatory, threatening, or di
sruptive conduct.

Project maintainers may clarify, correct, hide, or remove contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct or safety concerns privately to the maintainers listed in `SECURITY.md`; reports will be handled with appropriate confidentiality.
