# MQTT Topics

Canonical topic templates:

- `ev/charger/{deviceId}/telemetry`
- `ev/charger/{deviceId}/status`
- `ev/charger/{deviceId}/heartbeat`
- `ev/charger/{deviceId}/command`
- `ev/parking/{deviceId}/status`
- `ev/parking/{deviceId}/command`

Telemetry generated by simulators must include `source: "demo"` and `isSimulated: true`. MQTT credentials are backend/device secrets and must never be exposed through Vite environment variables.
