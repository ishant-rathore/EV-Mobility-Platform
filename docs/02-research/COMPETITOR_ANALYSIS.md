## 1. Purpose

This document analyzes existing EV mobility, charging, navigation, parking and payment solutions.

The objective is to understand:

- Existing solutions
- Their strengths
- Their limitations
- Market gaps
- Our opportunity for differentiation

---

# 2. Competitor Categories

The platform competes across multiple categories rather than with one single product.

| Category | Example Type |
|---|---|
| EV Navigation | Route and charging planning |
| Charging Networks | Charger discovery and charging |
| EV Aggregators | Multiple charging networks |
| Parking Platforms | Parking discovery/reservation |
| Payment Platforms | Digital payment |
| Smart Parking | IoT parking infrastructure |
| Mobility Platforms | Integrated mobility services |

# 4. Existing Strengths

Existing solutions are generally strong at individual functions:

- Navigation
- Charger discovery
- Charging network management
- Parking
- Payments

However, these functions are often distributed across different platforms.
